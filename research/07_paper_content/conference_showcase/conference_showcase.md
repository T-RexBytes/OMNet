# Conference Showcase & Research Presentation Outline

## Slide / Poster Narrative Structure

1. **Problem Statement & Clinical Motivation:** High inter-observer variability in breast cancer biopsy grading; need for automated multi-scale triage.
2. **The Data Leakage Dilemma:** Demonstrating the impact of patient-grouped vs. randomized splits on BreakHis benchmark reliability.
3. **Proposed Method (OMNet-V3):** Dual-branch hybrid architecture combining EfficientNet-B0 and ViT-Tiny/16 with the Magnification-Aware Adaptive Fusion (MAF) module.
4. **Key Innovation (Scale Gating):** Visualizing the monotonic progression of $\alpha(m)$ across $40\text{X} \rightarrow 400\text{X}$.
5. **Experimental Results:** 5-Fold Stratified Group CV results table (**84.56% Binary Acc**, **80.32% Macro-F1**, **44.75% Subtype Weighted-F1**).
6. **Interpretability & Manifold Structure:** Presenting t-SNE cluster separations, ROC curves, and confusion matrices.
7. **Clinical Takeaway:** High sensitivity ($>97\%$) enables effective computer-aided screening triage while maintaining patient-level generalization.
